Elevated Faith

EF was founded by Josh Gander in his parent’s home in August 2015, just weeks after he had dropped out of college. He realized that over the past couple of years he had been living a life without purpose. Until one day he felt persuaded to listen to what God had been telling him and that was to positively influence and share the truth with others, but he didn’t know how he would do that. He had a business already, but this one would be one that drew people closer to God through it. He created jewellery and apparel. By 2018, his family moved into a new house, so the business continued in their basement. The growth was visible, so they moved the business to their first warehouse. On September 6th, 2023, EF Kids was launched. They sold onesies, sweat sets and their #1 denim jacket. January of 2024, they moved to a much bigger warehouse with offices. EFs mission is to spread hope, purpose and love through meaningful designs. The designs are to inspire others through faith. “Elevated Faith” reflected the Christian belief that growth never stops, so every bracelet and piece of clothing serves as a “reminder to deepen your relationship with God and boldly live out your faith”. Their vision for the company is to expand their ways of giving back to the needy by creating more charity opportunities. This is achieved by just purchasing an item of your choice from the website and that money will be donated to charity. EF’s apparel caters to anyone who has or wants to reconcile their relationship with God. Some items have some of your favourite scriptures written on them, but you also have the choice to request a customised scripture. They have designs available for kids, men and women.
